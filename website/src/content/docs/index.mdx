## Choose what you want to add

<div class="principles-grid">
  <article class="principle">
    <strong><code>Marker.markText</code></strong>
    <p>Add one or more lines of text. Adjust the font, color, background, shadow, angle, and position.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="principle">
    <strong><code>Marker.markImage</code></strong>
    <p>Add a logo, QR code, icon, or any other image on top of your photo.</p>
  </article>
  <article class="principle">
    <strong><code>Marker.mark</code></strong>
    <p>Add text and images together, then choose which one appears on top.</p>
  </article>
</div>

Everything runs on the device or in the browser. There is no account to create and no image upload. When you add several watermarks, items later in the list appear on top.

[Try the live Web SDK](/playground/), [install the library](/getting-started/), or go directly to [choosing an API](/guides/choose-an-api/).
